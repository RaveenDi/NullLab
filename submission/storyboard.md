This is a storyboard, not a claim that a video has been recorded. Capture the working local product and label recorded playback/time compression. Do not claim outside-user adoption or Glimmer deployment.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Manufacturing score and controlled result | “This quality model scores 0.983. Change how the evaluation is performed and it scores 0.139. What did the first number actually measure?” |
| 0:15–0:35 | Dataset source, author task, construction label | “Before submitting a result, an author needs to know whether the evaluation earned it. This is our constructed fault on real semiconductor measurements, not an accusation against a study.” |
| 0:35–1:00 | Choose pipeline, study brief and predictor roles; fresh audit and baseline | “Define the decision before trusting the score. NullLab pins the inputs and executes the starting protocol. Unknown facts ask questions; clarifications create new versions.” |
| 1:00–1:30 | Actual Spark plan and typed edit; guard and measured result | “Spark identifies synthetic resampling before validation. The control moves it inside training folds; the guard checks the change before running it.” |
| 1:30–2:00 | Wonder critique and population limitation | “Wonder challenges our interpretation: the scored population and class prevalence also change. NullLab retains that dispute. A dramatic difference is not permission to overclaim.” |
| 2:00–2:20 | Clean twin, honest NOT TESTED state, recorded review room | “On this clean twin the free rules propose no test. That is not a clean bill of health. Recorded checks and objections stay visible, with questions to resolve.” |
| 2:20–2:40 | Saved investigation, revision identity, recorded/live badge, correct ZIP export | “Recover the investigation and inspect exact inputs and tested evidence. Unmeasured outcomes get an explicit assessment. A changed dataset or declaration cannot inherit old numbers.” |
| 2:40–3:00 | Architecture, real model roles, links/build boundary | “Built during the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. Meta reasoning challenges the method; controlled execution makes the claim inspectable. NullLab: put the score to the test.” |

Record the actual product. Cut waiting with clear time-compression labels. Include captions, readable metrics and licensed/no music. Verify the final file duration and public video URL before submitting.
