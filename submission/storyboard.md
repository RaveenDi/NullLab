## Full narration transcript

Read the nine paragraphs in order, one per time window above. Square-bracket directions are deliberately absent: the following is the complete spoken text.

This model reports almost perfect average precision: zero point nine eight three. But what if the evaluation helped create that score?

I built NullLab for machine-learning authors who need to challenge an evaluation before trusting it. This uses real UCI semiconductor measurements with a resampling flaw we deliberately introduced.

Start with the decision: what are you predicting, when are measurements available, and who receives the predictions? Supply supported Python code, or prepare a numeric CSV baseline. Missing facts trigger questions before training. Clarifications create a new version.

Here, Muse Spark proposes a specific intervention: generate synthetic records only inside training folds, and score original held-out records. It returns typed operations. NullLab checks applicability, traces training and scoring rows, preserves the classifier, and executes both protocols on three fixed seeds.

Average precision changes from zero point nine eight three to zero point one three nine. But Muse Wonder disputes the interpretation: the scored population and prevalence also changed. We keep that objection. This is a protocol comparison, not a pure causal estimate of leakage.

The research room shows actual checks, model assessments, and measurements. Disagreements stay visible. A clean twin with no proposed control says not tested. A model's no-flaw judgment also stays unmeasured. Neither becomes a clean bill of health.

Each investigation keeps exact input hashes, tested edits, predictions, environment, and attempted calls. Reopen saved audits and export the evidence. If no control was measured, the export says so. Changed inputs cannot inherit old results.

New internal evaluation cases exposed a critic-contract defect. We preserved that failure, repaired it, and ran a separate confirmation. This is a narrow, tested author instrument; broad research accuracy and outside-user impact remain unproven.

No API key is needed for local rules and execution. Meta reasoning adds proposed controls and independent critique. Built for the Meta hackathon: NullLab. Put the score to the test.

## What earns attention

- **User value:** the author must decide whether an evaluation supports a claim and receives an inspectable next step or evidence package. No adoption or time-saving number is invented.
- **Meta contribution:** Spark proposes an experiment; Wonder independently challenges methodology and interpretation. The deterministic guard/executor supplies the measured numbers. No claim of quantified critic advantage.
- **Technical credibility:** visible typed operation, guard event, original/control results, preserved objection, exact input identity and actual exports.
- **Demo clarity:** one manufacturing investigation, an early numerical hook, one short input workflow, one understandable limitation, and a clear artifact at the end.

Avoid claims of autonomous scientific truth, general paper evaluation, empirical clean clearance, causal leakage magnitude, validated Glimmer/local reasoning, or guaranteed hackathon placement. The original pilot failures stay public; this video concentrates on the working bounded author workflow.
