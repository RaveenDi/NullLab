## Project description

Before an ML author submits a result, NullLab tests the evaluation behind the score. The author supplies a supported Python pipeline or creates a numeric CSV baseline. NullLab reproduces the starting evaluation, uses typed controls to challenge a specific assumption, executes the control, and exports an input-bound evidence package. It distinguishes an untested judgment, a measured effect, an unsupported control and a disputed interpretation.

The question/upload-first Research Workdesk preserves original materials and a research thread, then captures decision time, population and predictor roles before training. A reviewed plan binds dispatch to the saved input revision. Unknown facts ask questions; unsupported generated group/future tasks stop before fitting. Authors clarify through new versions, reopen saved investigations, inspect recorded model contributions and export an honest assessment even when no control was measured. Revisions retain prior input/result identity; comparisons show metadata differences without claiming a causal ranking. This workflow has internal synthetic/API and actual desktop/mobile browser acceptance checks, not outside-user validation. Landing, recorded Evidence Lab and explanatory Architecture are separate from the actual workdesk.

Our manufacturing demonstration uses real UCI SECOM semiconductor measurements with an explicitly constructed resampling flaw. A controlled protocol scores original held-out records instead of synthetic evaluation records: PR-AUC changes from about 0.983 to 0.139 on three fixed seeds. The evaluation population and prevalence change, so we present a protocol comparison rather than claiming a pure causal amount of leakage. The original weak screening experiment and the clean twin's untested result are disclosed.

## Meta products and importance

Muse Spark 1.3 independently proposed the typed resampling intervention in the live demonstration. Muse Wonder first reviewed the original methodology independently, then challenged the interpretation of the measured change. That dispute is visible in the evidence. Meta reasoning chooses/challenges controls; deterministic validators, an executor and statistics supply numerical evidence. Model roles, attempts, token usage and input identity are recorded.

The transport also supports compatible local inference and explicit instance replay. Open-weight model quality has not been established, so no open-weight prize claim is made. The critic's additional value has not been quantified against a matched baseline; this is a demonstrated critique, not a claimed general reliability gain.

Two fixed internal synthetic cohorts exposed contract, transformer-tracing and scope-state defects. Their original coverage failures remain published separately from post-fix development regressions. The repaired workspace preserves missing-information questions, unsupported concerns and reviewer objections; these checks do not establish general benchmark accuracy or outside-user impact. A subsequent ten-case evaluation exposed a critic-contract ambiguity (0/3 flawed controls measured); its original failures remain intact. After the prompt repair, a separate six-case confirmation on new data measured 3/3 controls: disputed screening, no-effect scaling and a descriptive/inconclusive group split. Three clean cases received unmeasured no-flaw assessments. This is bounded internal coverage evidence, not general accuracy or a measured multi-agent benefit. See docs/EVAL-V2-PILOT.md, docs/EVAL-V2-FOLLOWUP.md and docs/EVAL-V2-RELEASE.md.

## Build boundary and limitations

Code, experiments, v2 safeguards and author workspace were built during the hackathon; the idea predates it. Third-party inputs are the licensed UCI dataset, sklearn/pandas/numpy/FastAPI and Meta inference. No external researchers or deployment impact have been measured. Arbitrary hostile hosted Python, general paper/research-gap evaluation and universally calibrated leakage inference are unsupported. The public view-only package executes no uploaded code; new audits run locally.
