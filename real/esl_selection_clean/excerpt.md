## Methods (excerpt)
We classify 50 samples (two equal classes) from 5000 quantitative measurements with a 1-nearest-neighbour classifier
built on the 100 measurements most correlated with the class labels. Error is estimated by 5-fold cross-validation;
the screening step is repeated inside every fold using only that fold's training samples.
## Results
The cross-validated error rate is close to 50%: the measurements carry no usable signal for the class labels.
