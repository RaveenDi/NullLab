WHAT STAYS VISIBLE

The limits travel with the result.

01

No test, no clearance.

A reproduced baseline, a model's no-flaw assessment and a measured control are different evidence states. Unsupported questions remain explicit.

02

Disagreement survives.

A critic's objection stays beside the result. Agreement does not establish scientific truth, and group/time measurements remain descriptive.

03

You review what leaves.

Audit prompts include source, claims, declarations, column names and aggregate diagnostics. CSV rows are not sampled into those prompts. Review private text before a model call.
