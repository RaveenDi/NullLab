02 / SCOPE & EVIDENCE

A precise conclusion
is a useful conclusion.

From claim to evidence →

A working instrument with an explicit boundary.

NullLab supports binary tabular ML investigations. A CSV creates a labelled generated baseline; reviewing an existing result requires its compatible pipeline and claim.

Questions, model judgments, measured effects and unresolved objections keep their own identities. An untested question never becomes a clean bill of health.

Our manufacturing demonstration uses real UCI SECOM records with a flaw we constructed. It is a transparent example, with its disputes and limitations preserved.
