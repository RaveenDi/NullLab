## Methods (excerpt)
We study whether a tree ensemble can flag samples with a high value of a continuous measurement from the remaining twelve measurements.
To enlarge the small sample, each record was perturbed eight times with 2% Gaussian noise.
The augmented pool was randomly divided into 75% training and 25% test records using a fixed random seed.
## Results
Our classifier achieves 96% accuracy on held-out data and generalizes to new samples.
